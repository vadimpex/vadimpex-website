What we trade

Phones, tablets, smart watches, laptops and accessories, across the brands the market actually asks for.

Stock reaches us through established distribution channels — factory lots via long-standing intermediaries, and stock traded within the European wholesale market.

Who we sell to

Wholesalers, independent shops and smaller companies. We work equally happily with a large distributor and a single store.

We do not sell to consumers. If you have arrived here looking for one phone for yourself, we are the wrong address — and we would rather say so now.
