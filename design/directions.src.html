for the new site

All three keep the existing logo, use your real red, and carry the same content in German and English. What differs is temperature: how formal the site feels, and how hard it leans on the one graphic idea your logo already contains.

Foundation 01

The mark is already a diagram

Your logo's triangle is drawn as a fan of fine lines converging on a single point. Read it as a map and it describes the business exactly: stock arriving from many manufacturers and markets, meeting at one warehouse in Baden, going back out again. Every direction below uses that fan as its graphic language — no stock photography required.

Foundation 02

Colour & type

Red sampled directly from your logo file. The bright red carries display type and graphics; the deeper red does the work on buttons and links, where the bright one fails contrast rules. Neutrals are warmed very slightly toward the red so nothing reads as default grey.

What doesn't change

The direction only decides how it looks. The structure and content are the same either way.

Pages
One scrolling homepage, plus Gewährleistung & Rücksendungen and Impressum as their own pages — as you asked, between a one-pager and a two-page site.
Languages
German and English, every page, with a proper language switch and correct hreflang tags.
Carried over
The company history, the warranty policy you already wrote, the Baden address, phone and fax, and the trading-platform memberships.
Dropped
Testimonials, events & exhibitions, the B2B portal, team photographs, the currency switcher, and the "SHOP" placeholder identity.
Added
A compliant Impressum, honest metadata, a working sitemap, and a year that updates itself.
Hosting
Static site on GitHub Pages, same as wts.vadimpex.com. No Bizzio, no monthly fee, no server to patch.

Team photographs are out, as you said. Direction B still reserves an image slot — if you ever photograph the warehouse or the building, it drops straight in without a redesign.
