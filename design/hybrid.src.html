      If you write to us or use the contact form, we process your name, email address and whatever you put in your message, for the sole purpose of answering you and, where it leads to business, managing that relationship.       From your documents

      What the papers changed

      Four things came out of the Firmenbuchauszug and Gewerbeschein that neither of us had right.

      Register court
      Not Baden, and not Handelsgericht Wiener Neustadt. It is Landesgericht Wiener Neustadt — the company moved there from Handelsgericht Wien on 12.11.2016.
      Registered name
      The Firmenbuch says VADIMPEX Handelsges.m.b.H., not "Handels GmbH". The Impressum has to use the registered wording. Your branding can stay VADIMPEX either way.
      Supervisory authority
      Bezirkshauptmannschaft Baden, confirmed on the Gewerbeschein. GISA 24185264, trade licence in force since 24.07.1995.
      No ODR link
      The EU online dispute resolution regulation was repealed on 20 July 2025. Many templates still carry that link; it must now be removed. Ours does not have it.
      The brand red
      The Illustrator files say #E31F28. The red I sampled earlier, #F83018, was JPEG noise in the logo on your old site. The real one is slightly deeper — and it clears the accessibility contrast threshold, which the wrong one did not.
      Drop ?preview=1
      Your Z Empire link carries a WordPress preview parameter. The page works fine without it, and preview links can behave differently for logged-out visitors — so the clean address is the safer one to publish.
      Two profiles say the wrong thing
      Z Empire lists you as "VADIMPEX Handles GmbH" — a typo for Handels. gsmExchange still says you are based in Vienna, though you moved to Baden in 2016. Both are worth a message to the platforms.
      gsmExchange since 2003
      Your gsmExchange profile shows member since 2003, premium since 2006. That is a genuinely strong signal in this trade, so it is now on the card rather than a UUID.

      Your documents are from 2016. They are ten years old, and an Impressum has to be current. Before this page goes live, pull a fresh Firmenbuchauszug and a current GISA extract. If anything changed — the registered name, the address, the managing director — the page changes with it.

      Two details must never reach the website. The extracts contain Hristo's private home address and his social insurance number. Neither belongs in an Impressum, and I have kept both out. Please store those files somewhere non-public.

      Confirm the exact chamber group. WKÖ and WKNÖ Sparte Handel are certain; the precise Fachgruppe wording should be copied from your entry in the WKO Firmen A-Z directory.
